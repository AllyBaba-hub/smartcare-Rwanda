FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SmartCare Rwanda?

SmartCare Rwanda is a digital patient self-registration and hospital navigation concept designed to simplify the journey from registration to care.

Do I have to register before visiting a hospital?

Pre-registration is designed to make your visit easier. Availability and final appointment confirmation depend on the participating healthcare facility.

How does the helpdesk confirmation work?

After the request is processed, the prototype workflow provides for an automated email confirmation followed by a support call from the Opkode X Helpdesk.

Can I register a dependent?

Yes. The registration workflow supports dependent information such as children or other eligible family members.
